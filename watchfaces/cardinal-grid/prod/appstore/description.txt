Cardinal Grid is a small cartographic orientation instrument for your wrist: range rings, radial grid lines, N/E/S/W, a fixed heading pointer and a level bubble over a contoured map. Turn your wrist and the geographic frame stays put while the watch turns beneath it.

The time sits in the upper left; the upper right says exactly what the compass knows: MAG with a bearing when calibrated, CAL while it is still calibrating, and NO MAG when the magnetometer is unavailable. Nothing is drawn as if it were known when it isn't.

Tap the watch to set the current heading as a reference (the ring flashes amber); tap twice to clear it. The centre dot is a bubble level.

Settings: clock format (follow the watch, 12-hour or 24-hour) and the colour of the time digits.
